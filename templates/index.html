Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SkillRock courses, projects, and certifications.

Are the courses on SkillRock free to access?
Yes! You can browse, watch, and learn from our curated technology courses completely free. You can also upgrade to Bhavishya 1.0 or special certifications whenever you need personalized AI teaching and mentor code reviews.
How does the 80% video watch milestone requirement work?
To maintain educational integrity, students must watch at least 80% of the course lectures before submitting their capstone milestone project. Our custom embedded player tracks elapsed watch time automatically while preventing unauthorized skipping.
What is Bhavishya 1.0 and how is it different?
Bhavishya 1.0 is our flagship AI-powered career preparation program. It features 19 comprehensive subjects taught by an interactive AI teacher that explains concepts step-by-step, asks interactive comprehension questions, and tracks your retention.
How are project submissions evaluated?
Submissions are graded against our official 100-point rubric: Core Functionality (40%), Code Architecture (30%), Live Deployment & Automated Tests (20%), and Documentation/README (10%).
How can recruiters and employers verify my certificate?
Every certificate includes an instant QR code and verification link at https://skillrock.in/verify/<cert_id>. Anyone can scan or visit the URL to verify the student's name, issued date, capstone deliverable, and cryptographic SHA-256 digital signature.
